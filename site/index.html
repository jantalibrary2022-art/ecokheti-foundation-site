How it works

From kitchen waste to living soil

A large share of what a city household throws away is kitchen and garden waste. Composted at home or in the neighbourhood, it never reaches a landfill and goes back into the soil as food for plants.

See our work
Photo: composting bin at a household
Photo: training session with residents

Separate

Households keep wet and dry waste apart at source.

Compost

Wet waste goes into home or community composting bins, following a written procedure.

Grow

The compost feeds rooftop gardens, kitchen gardens and farms.

Measure

We record what is diverted and grown, and publish what we learn.

What we do

Three areas of work

Waste and composting

Source segregation and decentralised composting for households, colonies, wards and campuses.

Climate-smart farming

Rooftop and kitchen gardens in towns, and organic, water-saving and soil-restoring practices with farmers.

Research and advisory

Studies on climate impacts on vulnerable communities, and advice on waste systems and carbon footprint.
