Our work

Our approach

Working models people can copy

We start with awareness and training, set up working units, write the procedure down, and step back once people run it themselves.

  • Awareness sessions and training for residents, schools and community groups
  • Demonstration units and model farms that people can see and copy
  • Standard operating procedures so practices continue after we step back
  • Partnerships with municipal bodies, government, research institutions and CSR funders

Programmes

Three programmes

Waste and composting

Source segregation and decentralised composting for households, residential colonies, wards and campuses, with bins, training and a written operating procedure for each site.

Climate-smart farming

Rooftop and kitchen gardens in towns, and organic, regenerative and water-saving practices with farmers, supported by demonstration plots.

Research and advisory

Studies on climate impacts on vulnerable communities, and advisory work on waste systems, carbon footprint and climate-smart agriculture.
