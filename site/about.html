About us

Who we are

A not-for-profit for climate action in Jharkhand

EcoKheti Foundation is a not-for-profit company incorporated on 5 May 2025 under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office in Ranchi. Our objects extend to the whole of India.

We work on climate change mitigation and adaptation through composting and waste management, climate-smart farming in towns and villages, and research that helps communities and policy makers act on evidence.

Our relationship with अर्बन-Kheti

अर्बन-Kheti is a climate-focused social enterprise in Ranchi, run as a business. EcoKheti Foundation is its not-for-profit counterpart and a separate legal entity, with its own registrations, bank account and books of account.

Grants and donations to the foundation are used only for its charitable objects. Where the two work together, the terms are set out in a written agreement.

Our objects

What we were set up to do

Our Memorandum of Association sets out ten objects. In summary:

  • Promote climate change mitigation and adaptation through awareness programmes, campaigns, training and public engagement in urban and rural areas
  • Conduct research and advisory work on environmental conservation, carbon footprint reduction, climate-smart agriculture, renewable energy and sustainable urban farming
  • Develop and spread affordable technologies and models that cut emissions, conserve resources and support eco-friendly livelihoods, particularly for marginalised people
  • Promote organic and regenerative farming, water conservation, soil health, agroforestry and sustainable land management
  • Work with government, research institutions, companies and civil society on climate action
  • Advocate for climate justice, with a focus on smallholder farmers, women and youth
  • Implement projects supported by national and international organisations, CSR funders and government
  • Publish reports, manuals and case studies on climate action and sustainable agriculture
  • Raise awareness of waste management, green commuting, energy conservation and sustainable consumption
  • Set up demonstration centres, model farms and innovation hubs for climate-resilient practices
